Careers

Build a region. Build a career.

We're a self-performing contractor. We run our own crews and our own plant, and we've been building for three decades across three countries. If you're an engineer, a site hand or a partner who takes the work seriously, there's a place for you here.

Why Current

Serious work for serious builders

  • Real projects with your name on them: courts, mosques, factories for global brands, and infrastructure in the New Capital
  • Work across Egypt, Saudi Arabia and Libya
  • We build with our own crews and plant, so you're on the job, not watching from the sidelines
  • Blue-chip clients and international standards on every site
  • You keep learning and moving up, whatever your level
  • Safety comes first here, because it's the people on site who matter most
Our culture

What it's like to work here

01

We work as one crew

Different trades, different backgrounds, one team. We pool what we each know and get the job done together.

02

We keep our word

We're straight with clients, partners and each other. We're honest about where things stand and we own our mistakes.

03

Safety first, every day

We run tight safety procedures and stay ahead of the risks, so everyone on site goes home in one piece.

04

We help you grow

You keep learning on the job. As you get better, so does the company, and there's room to move up.
